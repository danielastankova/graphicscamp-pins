Privacy Policy - GraphicsCamp Pinterest Poster

Last updated: 12 November 2026

GraphicsCamp Pinterest Poster ("the app") is a private, single-user tool operated by GraphicsCamp, a small Etsy shop that sells printable routines and calm-down tools for kids (graphicscamp.etsy.com). The app publishes GraphicsCamp's own product pins to GraphicsCamp's own Pinterest business account on a weekly schedule. It is not offered to other Pinterest users.

What Pinterest data the app accesses

  • Basic account information of the connected GraphicsCamp Pinterest account (username, account type), to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The account's own boards (names and IDs), to put each pin on the correct board.
  • The pins the app creates (pin ID), to avoid publishing the same pin twice.

The app does not read, collect or store data about other Pinterest users, followers, audiences or analytics.

How the data is used and stored

  • Authentication uses Pinterest OAuth only. The app never asks for or stores Pinterest passwords or session cookies.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the shop owner's own computer or as encrypted secrets of the shop's private automation, and are used only to call the Pinterest API on behalf of the GraphicsCamp account.
  • A small log of created pin IDs is kept so that no pin is published twice.
  • No Pinterest data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when access is revoked. The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest settings (Settings > Security and logins > Apps). Pin logs are deleted after 90 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact GraphicsCamp through the Etsy shop page graphicscamp.etsy.com ("Message seller").
